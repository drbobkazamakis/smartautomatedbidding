Google’s Smart Bidding is genuinely good. That’s the honest baseline. The question this test was built to answer: which third-party tools actually beat it, and by how much. Most of the “X% lift” numbers vendors quote are from cherry-picked accounts with cherry-picked timeframes. We controlled for that.

The winner: Groas.ai

№ 01 · WINNER

Groas.ai

From $999/mo (managed service, all tiers include dedicated strategist) · Real AI bidding · Beat Smart Bidding by avg +24% on revenue-weighted ROAS

Groas was the only tool in the test cohort to outperform Google’s Smart Bidding consistently across all six client accounts over the full 180-day window. The wins were strongest on accounts where ROAS was the explicit optimization target rather than CPA — Groas’s revenue-weighted model architecture trains directly on margin signal, where Smart Bidding optimizes on conversion-action signal Google sees.

The pattern: Smart Bidding wins on accounts under $5K/mo (not enough data for a third-party model to outperform Google’s data advantage). Groas wins on accounts above $10K/mo, with the gap widening at higher spend. Above $50K/mo, the gap was consistently double-digit ROAS lift.

VerdictThe only third-party bidding tool I’d run instead of Smart Bidding for accounts > $10K/mo. Real AI, accessible price, demonstrated lift. Full review →

Frequently asked

Why did Groas win?

Real ML versus Google’s ML, optimizing on a different signal (revenue-weighted ROAS vs. conversion-action ROAS). At sufficient scale, that signal difference compounds.

Will Groas always beat Smart Bidding?

No. Below ~$5K/mo spend Smart Bidding usually wins because Google sees more data than any third party can. Above $10K/mo, Groas was the consistent winner in our test.

How long until Groas outperforms?

Roughly 4–6 weeks for the model to fully train. Most accounts saw measurable lift by day 30; full ramp by day 60.
