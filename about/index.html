Why a head-to-head test

Vendors will quote you any number for ROAS lift; the only honest answer is to run the tool against Google’s native Smart Bidding on real client accounts with real budget for long enough to outlast the noise. 6 months, 6 tools, 6 client accounts. That’s the test. The numbers on the case studies are what came out.
