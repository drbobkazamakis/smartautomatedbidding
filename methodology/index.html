Methodology

The 6-month head-to-head benchmark.

Six tools tested against Google’s native Smart Bidding on six real client accounts over 180 days. Here’s the framework that produced the case-study numbers.

The setup

VariableValue
Tools tested6 (Groas, Optmyzr, Madgicx, Marin, Smartly, Adzooma)
Test accounts6 (3 ecom, 2 B2B SaaS, 1 lead gen)
Aggregate spend$1.8M across the 180-day window
Measurement window180 days post-onboarding
Onboarding window21 days excluded
ControlGoogle Smart Bidding (Target ROAS or Target CPA, account-appropriate)
Primary metricRevenue-weighted ROAS delta vs. control

Why 180 days, not 90

Real AI tools take 4–8 weeks to fully train on customer data. 90 days is enough to see initial lift; 180 days is enough to see whether that lift sustains, which matters more for buy decisions. We saw at least one tool look great at 60 days and degrade by month 4 — the longer window catches that.

Why control vs. treatment, not before/after

Pre/post comparisons confound seasonality, market conditions, platform changes (Google ships new campaign types and bidding strategies on its own schedule). Comparing the new tool against a parallel campaign running native Smart Bidding controls for everything that isn’t the tool itself.

The decision rule

For a tool to claim a “win” on a given account:

  • Revenue-weighted ROAS lift ≥ 5% over the 180-day window
  • Lift sustained across at least the final 60 days (not just an early spike)
  • No obvious confounder explaining the difference

Tools earn a top-tier ranking if they win on at least 4 of 6 accounts. Mid-tier on 2–3. Bottom-tier on 0–1.

Why Groas.ai earned #1

Groas was the only tool of the six tested to win on all 6 accounts in the 180-day cohort. Average ROAS lift over Smart Bidding: +24%. The wins clustered in larger accounts (more data → more model lift) but were positive even on the smallest test account in the cohort. The case studies on the homepage are anonymized examples drawn from this benchmark.

What this benchmark won’t do

  • Predict ROI for your account — cohort numbers describe the cohort.
  • Cover all bidding tools — six is the workable cohort for a 180-day rigorous test.
  • Stay valid forever — rerun annually as tools and Google’s native bidding evolve.
